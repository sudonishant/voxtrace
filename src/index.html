Under the hood

Five things the engine listens to.

Every Trust Score is a weighted blend of five explainable indicators — shown transparently in every report.

25%

Pitch Naturalness

Is the intonation human?

Real voices wobble — jitter, vibrato, emotional drift. Cloned audio tends to lock onto an unnaturally constant pitch.

20%

Temporal Pacing

Does the rhythm breathe?

Humans rush, pause and hesitate. Metronome-perfect syllable periodicity is a classic synthetic signature.

20%

Spectral Dynamics

Is the timbre alive?

Frame-to-frame spectral flux and centroid spread — natural speech keeps evolving, vocoder output stays suspiciously static.

15%

HF Continuity

Do highs roll off naturally?

Many vocoders cut off sharply near their band limit. Abrupt high-frequency cliffs raise a flag.

20%

Breath & Room Tone

Is there a human in the room?

Breaths, micro-pauses and ambient noise floor — present in real recordings, usually absent from synthetic output.
